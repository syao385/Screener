## ⚠️ PERMANENT INSTITUTIONAL PROTOCOLS

1. **S-Curve Adoption Phases**:
   - `INFLECTION` (Penetration $<5\%$): Early capex explosion. Scale aggressively.
   - `MASS_ADOPTION` (Penetration $5\%–30\%$): Golden compounding era. Core platform anchors hold Tier 1 ($20\%$ NAV cap).
   - `MATURITY` (Penetration $30\%–60\%$): Competitive consolidation. Transition to cash-flow quality.
   - `SATURATION` (Penetration $>60\%$): Cyclical peak. Exit or trim.
2. **Three Institutional Corrections**:
   - **Correction 1 (Depth over Breadth)**: 3 to 5 core companies deeply understood $>$ 1,000 tickers casually tracked.
   - **Correction 2 (High-Frequency Verification)**: Quarterly financials lag by 90 days; track monthly revenue disclosures (Taiwan MOPS) and hyperscaler capex guidance in real time.
   - **Correction 3 (Valuation Anchor)**: When $P/E$ exceeds historical mean $+3\sigma$, cap position size or trim $50\%$ into strength.
